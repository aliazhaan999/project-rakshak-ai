About PROJECT RAKSHAK AI

Our concept

To explore a simple, affordable rescue rover using manual movement control and proposed obstacle detection.

Our goals

Study robotics, emergency preparedness and the possible transport of essential food and health supplies.

Our team

Team Leader: Ali Azhaan
Team Partner: Aaron
School: Sunbeam School, Mau, Uttar Pradesh
